Data Protection & GDPR Compliance

Effective Date: November 2026

As an operation based in the United Kingdom, Retrospecty aligns data processing practices with standard data protection principles.

Controller

Bowley Design Ltd, registered in England & Wales 05762792, registered address c/o Brandon House, First Floor, 90 The Broadway, Chesham, Bucks HP5 1EG, UK. hello@bowleydesign.com

What we hold

Your email address, the business idea you type into the form, the preview and report we produce, and payment status. Card details go to Stripe and we never see them.

Why we use your data

To produce and send your preview and report, which is performing the service you asked for. To keep records of payments, which is a legal obligation. To run and improve the service, which is our legitimate interest.

Who processes your data

Stripe (payments), Resend (emails), Render (hosting), and Claude AI (research). Idea text is sent to the AI provider to produce the report.

Marketing

We add you to our mailing list only. Previews contain one payment link and nothing else.

Data retention

12 months for ideas and reports; payment records for the legally required period.

Your rights

To see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to take a copy. Email hello@bowleydesign.com. You can also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Personal and confidential information

Don't submit personal data about other people in your idea. Please don't include confidential information you can't risk sharing.

International transfers

Some processors are outside the UK.

Automated processing

Reports are written by an AI agent without human review, and it can make mistakes. Check the sources.

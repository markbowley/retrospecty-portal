About

Most startups don’t fail for new reasons

They fail for reasons someone else has already found out the hard way.

Retrospecty runs a pre-mortem on your idea before you spend the money. Tell it what you’re building and who it’s for. It finds companies that tried something similar and didn’t make it, then shows where your plan matches what went wrong for them.

Every case comes with its source. If a source can’t be verified, that case is dropped. If there aren’t at least three verified cases, you don’t get a report. You get an honest email saying there’s nothing worth charging for.

You get a free preview first and only pay if you want the full report.

This is learning from failure, as a service.

Retrospecty is run by Mo, an AI agent. Mo takes in the ideas, does the research, writes the reports and sends them out.

But there is a human-in-the-loop. Mark Bowley, a designer with 30 years of experience, built Mo and keeps an eye on things. Spending, pricing and anything published under the Retrospecty name need his sign-off.

It started as a three-week entry for a hackathon, built around one question: can an AI agent run a small business? The hackathon is over, and Retrospecty is continuing.
